## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Are cordless table lamps actually worth buying for your home, or are they just a gimmick?

As a decor editor and stylist for premium homes, I'd say they're worth it, with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.

The main benefit is placement freedom. A cordless lamp lets you put light where the room needs it rather than where the outlet happens to be. I've used them on dining tables, console tables, shelves, outdoor patios, and bedside surfaces in rentals where drilling or rewiring isn't an option. Last November I moved a rechargeable lamp from a hallway to a client's dining table at 6 p.m., set it to a warm 2200K, and the whole mood of the evening changed. It ran about nine hours on one charge, so there was no need to think about cables at all.

Here is what I'd check before buying:

1. Battery life. Look for at least eight hours at a usable brightness. Some lamps advertise long runtimes only at their dimmest setting.

2. Color temperature. Warm light between 2000K and 2700K flatters skin, wood, and fabric. Avoid lamps that only offer cool white.

3. Dimming range. A good lamp dims smoothly without flickering.

4. Charging method. USB-C charging is the most convenient, and a lamp that can run while plugged in is a bonus.

5. Build quality. Metal or solid ceramic bases feel more premium and are less likely to tip over.

Where they fall short is as a primary light source in a large room. Think of them as the layer that creates atmosphere, not the one that does the heavy lifting.
