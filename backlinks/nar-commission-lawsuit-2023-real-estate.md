## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What was the NAR commission lawsuit about and how did it affect the real estate industry?

The NAR lawsuit—officially Sitzer/Burnett v. NAR—was one of the most significant antitrust cases to hit real estate in decades. In October 2023, a Kansas City jury found the National Association of Realtors and several major brokerages liable for $1.78 billion in damages, which a judge later tripled to approximately $5.3 billion under antitrust law.

At its core, the lawsuit challenged how buyer-agent commissions were structured through the MLS system. For years, the system essentially locked in commission rates, and the plaintiff argued this artificially inflated costs for home buyers.

The industry impact has been substantial and immediate. Brokerages have begun restructuring how they handle buyer-agent compensation. The traditional model where seller's agents automatically offer buyer's agent commissions is being questioned and changed. Some brokerages have already shifted policies, and we're seeing more flexibility in how commissions are negotiated rather than assumed.

For buyers and sellers: this means more transparency and potentially lower costs, though it also means more negotiation required. For investors like myself, it's changed how we calculate deal costs and agent relationships.

The settlement also accelerated conversations about alternative commission models and technology platforms that could disrupt traditional brokerage structures.
